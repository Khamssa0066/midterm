Life of a Starfish

Starfish are beautiful animals that live in the ocean. They are also called sea stars. They have arms that usually grow around their central body. Most starfish have five arms, but some have more. They live in oceans around the world and can be found in different colours and sizes. Starfish move slowly using small tube feet. They eat small sea animals and help keep the ocean environment healthy. Some starfish can even grow a new arm if they lose one. They are interesting marine animals that play an important role in ocean life.

Facts About Starfish

  • Most starfish have five arms.
  • Starfish live in oceans around the world.
  • They move using small tube feet.
  • Some starfish can regrow lost arms.
